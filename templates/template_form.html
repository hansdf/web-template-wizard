Template Guidelines

Follow these best practices when creating templates:

  • Clear Names: Use descriptive names that explain the template's purpose
  • Structured Content: Organize content with proper formatting and sections
  • Reusable Elements: Include placeholders or variables where appropriate
  • Consistent Style: Maintain consistent formatting throughout

Tip: You can use markdown formatting in your templates for better organization.
